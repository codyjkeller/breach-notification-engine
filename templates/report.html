What this report does not cover

  • Whether the event legally counts as a "breach". Many laws excuse encrypted data or incidents with no risk of harm.
  • Notices to credit bureaus, which many states require for larger breaches.
  • Contract terms with customers and vendors, which are often shorter than any law.
  • Industry rules not listed here (for example banking regulators, state insurance laws, NYDFS).
  • Countries other than the United States, apart from the EU's GDPR.
